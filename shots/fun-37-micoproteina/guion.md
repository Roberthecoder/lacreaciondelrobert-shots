# Shot Fungi Mati #37 · Esa milanesa no es de pollo: la "carne" de hongo (micoproteína)

- **Serie:** Shot Fungi Mati · **Logo:** biología (ADN) · **Badge:** #37 · rama `pedido300/fun`.
- **Formato:** N1 "Por dentro" (macro: una milanesa en el plato → micro: tanque, hifas al microscopio, fibras → la misma milanesa, ya "transparente": hecha de hilos de hongo).
- **Gancho (de los 7):** #1 "Esto no es X: es Y" sobre lo que se ve: «Esa milanesa no es de pollo: es de hongo, hecha de hilos que tus ojos no pueden ver.» (combina con el #7, algo que tus ojos nunca van a ver).
- **Duración:** voz 997 caracteres / 178 palabras (≈ 58 s) + cierre 3.5 s → **61.5 s** en la base.
- **Voz:** pendiente de aprobación de Robert (~1000 créditos de ElevenLabs).
- **Tono:** curioso y ligero. No es consejo de nutrición ni publicidad de ninguna marca.

## Fuentes (cada dato se abrió en el texto completo el 29 oct 2026)

1. Finnigan, T. J. A. et al. (2019). Mycoprotein: The Future of Nutritious Nonmeat Protein, a Symposium Review. *Current Developments in Nutrition* 3(6): nzz021. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6554455/
   > "scientists collected and tested >3000 soil organisms from around the world until they discovered that Fusarium venenatum, a filamentous microfungus found originally in a field in Buckinghamshire, UK, made this conversion"
   > "via the continuous fermentation of F. venenatum followed by steaming, chilling, and freezing of the RNA-reduced biomass"
   > "When examined under a microscope mycoprotein that has been prepared in this way has a texture similar to that of chicken breast"
   > "Since a small amount of egg albumin is added as a binder at the end of processing, Quorn foods cannot be classed as vegan"
   > "The dietary fiber in mycoprotein is naturally occurring and is comprised of approximately one-third chitin (N-acetylglucosamine) and two-thirds β-glucan"
2. Wiebe, M. G. (2004). Quorn™ Myco-protein – Overview of a successful fungal product. *Mycologist* 18(1): 17–20. doi:10.1017/S0269915X04001089. https://davidmoore.org.uk/assets/fungi4schools/Reprints/Mycologist_articles/Post-16/Foods/V18pp017-020_Quorn.pdf
   > "more than 3000 fungal isolates from around the world were analysed for protein content, toxin production and suitable growth and morphology"
   > "The decision to develop a meat substitute based on fungal mycelium was made by the British company Rank Hovis McDougall (RHM) during the 1960s"
   > "A completely defined medium, based on glucose and ammonium (and supplemented with biotin), is used for the production of myco-protein."
   > "F. venenatum A3/5 has a specific growth rate (µ) of 0.17 to 0.20 h-1 (i.e. a doubling time between 3.5 and 4.1 h)"
   > "demonstrated that myco-protein could be consumed by lab animals or human volunteers without harmful effects if the RNA content of the cells was reduced"
   > "heating the mycelia in a separate tank to temperatures above 68°C (optimum 72-74°C) for 30 to 45 minutes"
   > "A filamentous fungus was chosen for the production of this meat substitute, since it was believed that the mycelia would add a fibrous texture, comparable to that of meat"
   > "Quorn™ has been commercially available in the UK since 1985"
3. Okeudo-Cogan, M. C. et al. (2023). Understanding the microstructure of a functional meat analogue: Demystifying interactions between fungal hyphae and egg white protein. *Food Hydrocolloids* 140: 108606. https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/197030/
   > "The fungal filaments, or hyphae, typically have cross-sectional diameters of 3 to 5 μm and lengths of 400 to 700 μm (Finnigan, 2011)"
   > "Relatively unbranched flexible hyphae with high aspect ratios give MYC increased mechanical strength and the potential to form a hierarchal fibre arrangement analogous to the muscle fibres in mammalian muscle tissue"
   > "in order to optimize the required texture, additional proteins generally have to be included, typically egg white protein (EWP)"
4. Monteyne, A. J. et al. (2020). Mycoprotein ingestion stimulates protein synthesis rates to a greater extent than milk protein in rested and exercised skeletal muscle of healthy young men: a randomized controlled trial. *Am J Clin Nutr* 112(2): 318–333. doi:10.1093/ajcn/nqaa092. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32438401/ (Universidad de Exeter; un coautor es de Marlow Foods, la empresa de Quorn.)
