# Shot Fungi Mati #27 · Ese hongo amarillo no está matando a tu planta: *Leucocoprinus birnbaumii*

- **Serie:** Shot Fungi Mati · **Logo:** biología (ADN) · **Badge:** #27 · rama `pedido300/fun`.
- **Formato:** N1 Por dentro (macro → micro → macro): la maceta de tu casa → la tierra al microscopio (micelio, esporas, pigmento) → la misma maceta, ya entendida.
- **Gancho (de los 7):** #1 "Esto no es X: es Y" sobre lo que se ve en pantalla: «Ese hongo amarillo en tu maceta no está matando a tu planta: se come lo que ya está muerto.»
- **Duración:** voz 999 caracteres / 177 palabras (≈ 58 s) + cierre 3.5 s → **61.5 s** en la base.
- **Voz:** pendiente de aprobación de Robert (~999 créditos de ElevenLabs).
- **Tono:** tranquilizador y útil, con un truco para hacer en casa. No es consejo médico.

**Música:** cama grave, cálida y continua, sin golpe; efectos en el mismo cuadro: "pop" suave al brotar cada hongo, arena al vaciar el costal, viento leve con la espora, crujidos al deshacerse las hojitas, gotas de riego, burbujeo del matraz, sello "NO SE COME", tics de la noche que pasa y campanita al revelar la huella de esporas.

Barra de escala: 5 cm (maceta), 2 cm (hongo), 1 mm (micelio en la tierra), 10 µm (esporas, en el recuadro); las moléculas son esquema, sin barra.

## Fuentes (consultadas el 29 oct 2026; de Bartsch 2005 solo se vieron el título y la ficha de PubMed, no el texto completo)

1. Iowa State University Extension and Outreach, Yard and Garden. "Will yellow mushrooms harm my houseplant?" (actualizado 14 ene 2025). https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/faq/will-yellow-mushrooms-harm-my-houseplant
   > "The small, yellow mushroom is probably Leucocoprinus birnbaumii. The fungus is sometimes referred to as yellow houseplant mushroom, yellow parasol, or flower pot parasol."
   > "A mushroom is the reproductive structure of a fungus that spends the rest of its life cycle as a thread-like body in the soil or debris, not usually visible. That thread-like body, called a mycelium, could have been introduced to the pot in the potting mix. Alternately, a spore of the fungus (similar to a seed) may have floated through the air and landed in the pot"
   > "The Leucocoprinus birnbaumii fungus breaks down dead organic matter in the potting soil. It does not harm living plants"
   > "the mushrooms are regarded as poisonous to people and animals. If pets or small children reside in the home, it would be wise to remove the mushrooms as they appear. Fungicide treatments are generally not effective against mushrooms."
   > "The small, lemon yellow mushrooms are about 1 to 3 inches tall with 1 to 2 inch oval or bell-shaped caps."
2. Madeiras, A. (2021). "Mushrooms in Potted Plants". Greenhouse Updates, 3 sep 2021. UMass Extension, University of Massachusetts Amherst. https://www.umass.edu/agriculture-food-environment/greenhouse-floriculture/greenhouse-updates-september-3-2021
   > "These mushrooms are typically harmless to plants and feed on organic matter in the potting media."
   > "Yellow parasols have a bright yellow cap when young, but fade to white as they mature. Caps are textured on top and the gills on the underside are free … There is a ring around the stipe (stalk) below the cap."
   > "The caps produce abundant white spores, which may be visualize by leaving the cap overnight on a dark surface."
   > "their exact level of toxicity is unknown and they should not be eaten."
   > "Avoid overwatering. The potting medium may be replaced, but once the fungus is established it can be difficult to get rid of."
