## Fuentes (cada cifra se abrió en la fuente original el 28 oct 2026; detalle en `verificacion.md`)

1. **Kareklas, K., Nettle, D. y Smulders, T. V. (2013).** Water-induced finger wrinkles improve handling of wet objects. *Biology Letters* 9: 20120999. Newcastle University. PMC3639753.
   > "Twenty participants… manipulated submerged and dry objects…" · "With wrinkled fingers, transfer of submerged objects happened in 12 per cent (±2.3% s.e.m.) less time than with unwrinkled fingers. There was no difference in the time it took to transfer dry objects with or without wrinkles" (2013)
   > "This wrinkling was previously thought to be the result of osmotic swelling of the stratum corneum… it is in fact due to a reduction in the volume of the fingertip pulp, caused by vasoconstriction, which in turn is controlled by the autonomic nervous system"
2. **Haseleu, J., Omerbašić, D., Frenzel, H., Gross, M. y Lewin, G. R. (2014).** Water-induced finger wrinkles do not affect touch acuity or dexterity in handling wet objects. *PLoS One* 9: e84949. Max Delbrück Center, Berlín. PMC3885627.
   > "Human non-hairy (glabrous) skin of the fingers, palms and soles wrinkles after prolonged exposure to water." · "(n = 40)… We found that skin wrinkling did not improve dexterity in handling wet objects" (2014)
3. **Wilder-Smith, E. P. y Chow, A. (2003).** Water-immersion wrinkling is due to vasoconstriction. *Muscle & Nerve* 27: 307–311. PMID 12635117.
   > "Wrinkling was accompanied by significant reduction in blood flow velocity in all vessels, with a maximum in digital vessels."
4. **Fustes, O. J. H. et al. (2026).** The skin-wrinkling test: principles and clinical applications. *Arq. Neuropsiquiatr.* 84(7). PMC13472706 (revisión).
   > "in sympathetically-denervated areas (for example, following median or ulnar nerve injury), wrinkling does not occur… If the sympathetic nervous system is blocked (through local anesthesia or sympathectomy), wrinkling likewise disappears"

## Notas
- El "para qué" se dice como pregunta abierta: el estudio de 2013 encontró ventaja y el de 2014, con la misma prueba, no.
- El año de los primeros estudios (1936, Lewis y Pickering) se quitó porque el original no está abierto en línea.
- El corte de la yema y los vasos son esquemáticos (la escala de 1 mm es aproximada).
