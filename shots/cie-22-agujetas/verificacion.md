# Verificación de hechos · Shot Científico #22 · Las agujetas no son ácido láctico

- **Fecha:** 29 oct 2026. **Quién verificó:** subagente verificador independiente (no escribió el guion).
- **Método:** resúmenes leídos de la fuente original (Europe PMC/Crossref) y textos completos abiertos (Frontiers, PMC). No se usaron resúmenes del buscador como fuente.

## Fuentes consultadas

- **[S83a]** Schwane JA, Watrous BG, Johnson SR, Armstrong RB. *Is Lactic Acid Related to Delayed-Onset Muscle Soreness?* The Physician and Sportsmedicine 11(3): 124–131 (1983). doi:10.1080/00913847.1983.11708485 (PMID 27409551). **Es el estudio que midió el lactato.**
- **[S83b]** Schwane JA, Johnson SR, Vandenakker CB, Armstrong RB. *Delayed-onset muscular soreness and plasma CPK and LDH activities after downhill running.* Med Sci Sports Exerc 15(1): 51–56 (1983). doi:10.1249/00005768-198315010-00010 (PMID 6843319). Mismo diseño (plano vs. bajada), pero **no mide lactato**.
- **[CH03]** Cheung K, Hume P, Maxwell L. *Delayed onset muscle soreness: treatment strategies and performance factors.* Sports Med 33(2): 145–164 (2003). PMID 12617692.
- **[HO18]** Hotfiel T et al. *Advances in Delayed-Onset Muscle Soreness (DOMS): Part I: Pathogenesis and Diagnostics.* Sportverletz Sportschaden (2018). PMID 30537791.
- **[HY19]** Hody S et al. *Eccentric Muscle Contractions: Risks and Benefits.* Front Physiol 10: 536 (2019). doi:10.3389/fphys.2019.00536.
- **[MT16]** Mizumura K, Taguchi T. *Delayed onset muscle soreness: Involvement of neurotrophic factors.* J Physiol Sci (2016). PMID 26467448.
- **[MH03]** McHugh MP. *Recent advances in the understanding of the repeated bout effect.* Scand J Med Sci Sports 13: 88–97 (2003). PMID 12641640.
- **[MT03]** McHugh MP, Tetro DT. *Changes in the relationship between joint angle and torque production associated with the repeated bout effect.* J Sports Sci (2003). PMID 14626372.
- **[SO22]** Sonkodi B. *Should We Void Lactate in the Pathophysiology of Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness? Not So Fast!* Metabolites 12(9): 857 (2022). PMC9505902.
