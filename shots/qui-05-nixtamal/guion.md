## Fuentes (cada dato y cifra se abrió en la fuente original el 28 oct 2026)

1. **Hervir en agua con cal (hidróxido de calcio) y reposar toda la noche; el pericarpio se va al lavar; nejayote; Aztecas** — Kamau E.H., Nkhata S.G., Ayua E.O. "Extrusion and nixtamalization conditions influence the magnitude of change in the nutrients and bioactive components of cereals and legumes." *Food Sci Nutr* 8(4):1753–1765 (2020). PMC7174222.
   > "boiling the corn in water containing lime (calcium hydroxide) … The boiled grain is then steeped overnight before it is washed and ground into corn masa" · "germ is never removed in the subsequent steps of washing during nixtamalization unlike the pericarp" · "the nixtamalization wastewater ("nejayote")" · "Nixtamalization is an ancient method practiced by the Aztecs"
2. **El grano se queda con calcio** — Bressani R. et al. "Effect of processing conditions on phytic acid, calcium, iron, and zinc contents of lime-cooked maize." *J Agric Food Chem* 52(5):1157–62 (2004). PMID 14995114.
   > "Calcium content increased in whole maize, endosperm, and germ with lime concentration and cooking and steeping times."
3. **Niacina mejor aprovechada** — Suri D.J., Tanumihardjo S.A. "Effects of Different Processing Methods on the Micronutrient and Phytochemical Contents of Maize: From A to Z." *Compr Rev Food Sci Food Saf* 15(5):912–926 (2016). PMID 33401800.
   > "nixtamalization (soaking in alkaline solution) can increase bioavailability of riboflavin and niacin" · "In some cases, such as niacin, processing is actually necessary for nutrient bioavailability."
4. **Niacina "amarrada"; Mesoamérica; Europa sin el proceso** — "From forgotten to frontier: vindicating Latin America's indigenous biotechnology." *J Ethnobiol Ethnomed* (2025). PMC12288233.
   > "In Mesoamerica, the process of nixtamalization, soaking maize in lime (calcium hydroxide)…" · "This treatment releases bound niacin (vitamin B₃)" · "Without it, maize-based diets risk niacin deficiency and pellagra, a problem tragically evident in Europe, where maize was adopted without the corresponding Indigenous processing knowledge"
5. **Europa: maíz no nixtamalizado, pelagra y sus síntomas; masa sin cal que no amarra** — "Traditional Foods From Maize (Zea mays L.) in Europe." *Front Nutr* (2021). PMC8780548.
   > "the maize used in Europe is not nixtamalized … therefore, the paste does not reach a great cohesion and its texture is brittle and sandy" · "The introduction of maize into European diet was associated with specific nutrient deficiencies such as pellagra" · "Symptoms of pellagra include inflamed skin, diarrhea, dementia"
6. **Soweto 2025: fumonisina B1 −95 %** — Maris E. et al. "Nixtamalization of Maize to Reduce Mycotoxin Exposure: A Human Biomonitoring Intervention Study in Soweto, South Africa." *Toxins* 17(11):527 (2025). PMID 41295842.
   > "visibly moldy maize subjected to nixtamalization" · "reducing fumonisin B1 (FB1), fumonisin B2, and zearalenone (ZEN) by 95%, 95%, and 89%, respectively"

## Notas
- El dibujo es esquemático (el grano, la molécula de niacina y el "candado" son metáforas).
- No se da la concentración de cal ni recetas de cocina; no se dice que el proceso "cura" nada.
- La fumonisina es una toxina de hongos (micotoxina); en el estudio se midió en el maíz, y en la orina de los voluntarios no hubo diferencia contra el maíz de control: por eso la voz habla solo de la baja en el maíz.
