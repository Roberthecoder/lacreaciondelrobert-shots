   > "Mucormycosis is caused by molds belonging to the order Mucorales, most commonly *Rhizopus* species." · "Other causative species include *Mucor* species"
   > "Necrosis is the result of invasion of blood vessels, subsequent thrombosis, and tissue death."
   > "The lesions may appear red and indurated and often progress to black eschars." · "Necrotic black lesions on the hard palate or nasal turbinate"
   > "Mucormycosis primarily affects people who are immunocompromised and those with uncontrolled diabetes."
   > "Mucormycosis is acquired through inhalation, inoculation, or ingestion of spores from the environment."
   > "Most cases are sporadic, and mucormycosis is not contagious."
2. CDC. *About Mucormycosis*. https://www.cdc.gov/mucormycosis/about/index.html
   > "a serious but rare fungal infection" · "caused by a group of molds called mucormycetes" · "Mucormycetes are often found in soil and in decaying organic matter" (hojas, madera podrida) · "Mucormycosis does not spread from person to person." · "Usually, only people with weakened immune systems get mucormycosis."
3. Patel, A. et al. (2021). Multicenter Epidemiologic Study of Coronavirus Disease–Associated Mucormycosis, India. *Emerging Infectious Diseases* 27(9): 2349–2359. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8386807/ (datos de sept–dic 2020)
