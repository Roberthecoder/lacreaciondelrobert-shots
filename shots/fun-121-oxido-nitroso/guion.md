# Shot Fungi Mati #121 · Este costal no es solo comida para tu milpa

- **Título:** «Este costal no es solo comida para tu milpa | Shot Fungi Mati 121»
- **Serie:** Shot Fungi Mati · **Logo:** biología (ADN) · **Badge:** 121 (3 dígitos) · rama `claude/fun-120-124-suelo-clima`.
- **Formato:** N1 Por dentro + N2 El número. Viaje: costal de fertilizante y milpa (macro) → suelo encharcado sin oxígeno → hifa de
  *Fusarium* y su enzima (micro, fondo oscuro) → la misma milpa, con el gas subiendo (macro).
- **Gancho (de los 7):** #1 «Esto no es X: es Y» sobre lo que se ve (el costal), con el número que contradice (#4) dentro:
  «Este costal no es solo comida para tu milpa: parte se escapa como óxido nitroso, un gas que, kilo por kilo, calienta doscientas setenta y tres veces más que el CO₂.»
- **Matiz obligatorio:** la parte de los hongos varía mucho entre suelos y métodos; el 89 % es de UN pastizal y con inhibidores, que tienen límites.
  La voz lo dice («Ojo: fue un solo suelo…»). No decimos que los hongos sean «la» fuente principal del N₂O.
- **Voz:** 177 palabras (las cifras van escritas en letra) / 964 caracteres (≈ 58.0 s) + cierre 3.5 s → **61.5 s** estimados.
  Voz pendiente de aprobación de Robert.

## Fuentes (abiertas el 5 nov 2026)

1. **GWP del N₂O (273 a 100 años):** IPCC AR6 WG1 (2021), cap. 7, tabla 7.15 (la página del IPCC dio 403 desde aquí; lo confirma la EPA):
   EPA, *Understanding Global Warming Potentials*. https://www.epa.gov/ghgemissions/understanding-global-warming-potentials
   > "Nitrous Oxide (N2O) has a GWP 273 times that of CO2 for a 100-year timescale."
   > "The GWPs listed above are from the IPCC's Sixth Assessment Report, published in 2021."
   > "N2O emitted today remains in the atmosphere for more than 100 years, on average."
   Confirmado por el verificador en el PDF del IPCC AR6 WG1 cap. 7, tabla 7.15: fila N2O «GWP-100 273 ± 130» (vida media 109 ± 10 años).
2. **Ozono:** Ravishankara, A. R., Daniel, J. S. y Portmann, R. W. (2009). Nitrous oxide (N2O): the dominant ozone-depleting substance emitted
   in the 21st century. *Science* 326: 123–125. https://ozone.unep.org/sites/default/files/2019-08/Ravishankara%20et%20al_Science_N2O_10_2_09.pdf
   > "N2O emission currently is the single most important ozone-depleting emission and is expected to remain the largest throughout the 21st century."
   > "N2O is unregulated by the Montreal Protocol."
3. **Agricultura:** Tian, H. et al. (2020). A comprehensive quantification of global nitrous oxide sources and sinks. *Nature* 586: 248–256.
   https://escholarship.org/uc/item/9sp7r9qc
   > "Global human-induced emissions, which are dominated by nitrogen additions to croplands, increased by 30% over the past four decades to 7.3 (4.2–11.4) teragrams of nitrogen per year." (periodo 1980–2016)
4. **Desnitrificación fúngica (descubrimiento):** Shoun, H., Kim, D.-H., Uchiyama, H. y Sugiyama, J. (1992). Denitrification by fungi.
   *FEMS Microbiology Letters* 73(3): 277–281. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1426992/ (solo resumen)
   > (resumen) muchos hongos del grupo *Fusarium* reducen nitrito sin oxígeno a NO, N2O y/o N2; "nitrous oxide was the major product".
   **Para el verificador:** abrir el resumen; el volumen aparece distinto en algunas bases (73 vs 94).
5. **P450nor y la enzima que falta:** Higgins, S. A., Schadt, C. W., Matheny, P. B. y Löffler, F. E. (2018). Phylogenomics reveal the dynamic
   evolution of fungal nitric oxide reductases… *Genome Biology and Evolution* 10(9): 2474–2489. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6161760/
   > "P450nor, a heme-containing cytochrome P450, that catalyzes the two electron reduction of nitric oxide (NO) to N2O"
   > "fungi cannot convert N2O to inert N2 like many denitrifying bacteria"
   > "Despite extensive efforts, fungal contributions to N2O emissions remain uncertain."
   > streptomycin "only inhibits a fraction of the active bacteria present in soil" (límite de los inhibidores)
6. **207 hongos, 70 productores:** Maeda, K. et al. (2015). N2O production, a widespread trait in fungi. *Scientific Reports* 5: 9697.
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4403702/
   > "investigated the N2O-producing ability of a collection of 207 fungal isolates"; seventy strains producing N2O in pure culture, mostly Hypocreales (*Fusarium oxysporum*, *Trichoderma*)
   > strains "were previously isolated from agricultural soils or plant roots" (colección del INRA, Dijon, Francia)
   > "no nitrous oxide reductase has been identified in fungi"; "N2O was always the end-product of denitrification"
   > earlier studies "reported that fungi could contribute up to 18% of potential denitrification" (para «en otros… mucho menor»)
7. **El pastizal:** Laughlin, R. J. y Stevens, R. J. (2002). Evidence for fungal dominance of denitrification and codenitrification in a
   grassland soil. *Soil Science Society of America Journal* 66(5): 1540–1548 (resumen en AGRIS: https://agris.fao.org/search/en/records/65df185f0f3e94b9e5d4fe38).
   > "Cycloheximide decreased the flux of N2O by 89% and streptomycin decreased the flux by 23%, indicating that fungi were responsible for most of the N2O production."
   > Suelo de pastizal de Irlanda del Norte; un solo suelo; método de inhibición selectiva (SIRIN) + ¹⁵N.
