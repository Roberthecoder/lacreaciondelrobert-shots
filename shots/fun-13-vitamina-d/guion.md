## Fuentes (abiertas el 29 oct 2026)

1. Urbain, P. y Jakobsen, J. (2015). Dose–Response Effect of Sunlight on Vitamin D2 Production in *Agaricus bisporus*
   Mushrooms. *Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry* 63(37): 8156–8161. doi:10.1021/acs.jafc.5b02945.
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26314311/ (resumen completo leído). Autores: University Medical Center, Freiburg,
   Alemania, y National Food Institute, Technical University of Denmark, Copenhague, Dinamarca.
   > "The dose response effect of UV-B irradiation from sunlight on vitamin D2 content of sliced Agaricus bisporus (white button mushroom) during the process of sun-drying was investigated."
   > "During the first hour of sunlight exposure, the vitamin D2 content of the mushrooms increased in a linear manner, with concentrations increasing from 0.1 μg/g up to 3.9 ± 0.8 μg/g dry weight (DW). At the subsequent two measurements one and 3 h later, respectively, a plateau was reached. Two hours of additional exposure triggered a significant decline in vitamin D2 content."
   > "After just 15 min of sun exposure and an UV-B dose of 0.13 J/cm(2), the vitamin D2 content increased significantly to 2.2 ± 0.5 μg/g DW (P < 0.0001), which is equivalent to 17.6 μg (704 IU) vitamin D2 per 100 g of fresh mushrooms and comparable to levels found in fatty fish like the Atlantic salmon."
2. Cardwell, G., Bornman, J. F., James, A. P. y Black, L. J. (2018). A Review of Mushrooms as a Potential Source of
   Dietary Vitamin D. *Nutrients* 10(10): 1498. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6213178/
   > "As they are usually grown in darkness" … "is commonly reported to be less than 1 μg/100 g FW"
   > "sun-exposed sliced mushrooms produce more vitamin D2 than whole mushrooms"
   > "UV-exposed fresh mushrooms will retain nutritionally relevant amounts of vitamin D2 when refrigerated for one week or less"
   > "Vitamin D2 from mushrooms was as effective as supplemental vitamin D2 in raising and maintaining serum 25(OH)D2"
3. Keegan, R. J., Lu, Z., Bogusz, J. M., Williams, J. E. y Holick, M. F. (2013). Photobiology of vitamin D in mushrooms
   and its bioavailability in humans. *Dermato-Endocrinology* 5(1): 165–176. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3897585/
   > "When mushrooms are exposed to UV radiation, provitamin D2 is converted to previtamin D2. Once formed, previtamin D2 rapidly isomerizes to vitamin D2 in a similar manner that previtamin D3 isomerizes to vitamin D3 in human skin."
   > "ingestion of 2000 IUs of vitamin D2 in mushrooms is as effective as ingesting 2000 IUs of vitamin D2 or vitamin D3 in a supplement in raising and maintaining blood levels of 25-hydroxyvitamin D"
   > "are grown in the dark and therefore contain negligible concentrations of vitamin D2."
   > (sobre la membrana) "The planar structure of 7-DHC and provitamin D2 can fit in between the triglyceride side chains and polar head groups."
4. Jordá, T. y Puig, S. (2020). Regulation of Ergosterol Biosynthesis in *Saccharomyces cerevisiae*. *Genes* 11(7): 795.
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7397035/
   > "Ergosterol is an essential component of fungal cell membranes"
   > "Ergosterol, which is structurally analogous to cholesterol, has been used as a precursor of vitamin D2"
5. MedlinePlus (Biblioteca Nacional de Medicina de EE. UU.). Vitamin D. Revisión 21 ene 2025.
   https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/002405.htm
   > "19 to 70 years: 600 IU (15 mcg) per day"
   > "The body makes vitamin D when the skin is directly exposed to the sun."
   > (misma página: mayores de 70 años, 800 IU / 20 mcg al día)
   > "Some mushrooms you buy in the store have higher vitamin D content because they have been exposed to ultraviolet light."

Nota: el ergosterol es la "provitamina D2" (Keegan 2013 y Jordá y Puig 2020). La cifra de 17.6 µg es de vitamina D2 por
100 g de champiñón fresco, calculada por los autores a partir de 2.2 µg/g de peso seco (dato de 2015), con champiñón rebanado al sol de mediodía.
El estudio de Keegan et al. (2013) inscribió a 30 adultos sanos (25 terminaron, 3 meses, D2 de champiñón en cápsulas de polvo); la frase dice "suplemento de D2" porque
otro estudio citado por Cardwell 2018 no vio efecto y la D3 sube más que la D2.
