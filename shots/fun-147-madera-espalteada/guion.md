- **Serie:** Shot Fungi Mati · **Logo:** biología (ADN) · **Badge:** #147 · rama `claude/fun-145-149-objetos`.
- **Formato:** N1 · Por dentro. Viaje macro → micro → macro: una tabla de madera espalteada → dentro del tronco (hifas, choque, muro de melanina) → la misma tabla, ya "leída" como mapa de territorios.

Tema propio de 4 compases (motivo → respuesta) en el gancho; la secuencia/cadencia una octava abajo en las hifas; el tema regresa en el mapa; la respuesta arriba en el estudio; en el cierre el tema vuelve en **re mayor** y queda un acorde sonando. 1. Robinson, S. C. (2012). Developing fungal pigments for "painting" vascular plants. *Applied Microbiology and Biotechnology* 93: 1389–1394. doi:10.1007/s00253-011-3858-2 (Faculty of Forestry, University of Toronto; hoy Seri Robinson está en Oregon State University).
   > "The word was specifically defined by Robinson et al. (2007a) to include all types of pigment, both melanin and otherwise, produced on wood by fungi."
   > "The first category, bleaching, includes the lightening of the wood due to colonization and lignin removal by white rot fungi. In association with these white rot fungi, but in its own category, are zone lines, produced due to inter- or intrafungal antagonism. The third category is defined simply as pigmentation"
   > "Xylaria polymorpha is capable of creating zone lines without the antagonism of a secondary fungus"
   > "Chlorociboria sp. produces a blue/green pigment"
   > "the first noted use of fungal-pigmented wood is from the 1400 s in Renaissance Italy (Blanchette et al. 1992). During this time period, Italian artists utilized Populus sp. wood stained bluegreen by Chlorociboria sp., in intarsia wood panels."
   > "spalted wood particularly preferred for small furniture items, like coffee tables (Donovan and Nicholls 2003) and wood turnings (Lindquist 1977)."
