## Fuentes (abiertas el 5 nov 2026)

1. **Zalar, P., Novak, M., de Hoog, G. S. y Gunde-Cimerman, N. (2011).** Dishwashers – A man-made ecological niche accommodating human opportunistic fungal pathogens. *Fungal Biology* 115(10): 997–1007 (muestreo 2009–2010; Universidad de Liubliana, Eslovenia). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21944212/
   > "189 of which were sampled in private homes of 101 towns or communities." (102 Eslovenia, 42 resto de Europa, 13 Norteamérica, 3 Sudamérica, 5 Israel, 10 Sudáfrica, 7 Lejano Oriente, 7 Australia)
   > "Sixty-two percent of the dishwashers were positive for fungi, and 56% of these accommodated Exophiala."
   > "the black yeasts Exophiala dermatitidis and Exophiala phaeomuriformis … were persistently and most frequently isolated."
   > "high temperature, high moisture and alkaline pH values typically occurring in dishwashers can provide an alternative habitat"
