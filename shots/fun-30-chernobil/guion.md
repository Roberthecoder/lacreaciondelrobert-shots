1. Zhdanova, N. N., Zakharchenko, V. A., Vember, V. V. y Nakonechnaya, L. T. (2000). Fungi from Chernobyl: mycobiota of the inner regions of the containment structures of the damaged nuclear reactor. *Mycological Research* 104(12): 1421–1426. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0953756200002756 (resumen: https://aspergillus.org.uk/article_database/fungi-from-chernobyl-mycobiota-of-the-inner-regions-of-the-containment-structures-of-the-damaged-nuclear-reactor). Muestreo 1997–98. Grupo de Nelli Zhdanova, Instituto de Microbiología y Virología Zabolotny, Academia Nacional de Ciencias de Ucrania (Kiev) (afiliación confirmada en el registro de Cambridge: Institute of Microbiology and Virology, Ukrainian National Academy of Sciences, Kiev).
   > "Extensive fungal growth has been detected on the walls and other building constructions" (del Shelter del 4.º bloque, 1997–98)
   > "The mycobiota comprised 37 species of 19 genera."
   > "dominance of melanin-containing species in heavily contaminated sites"
   > *Cladosporium sphaerospermum* figura entre los hongos más comunes.
2. Dadachova, E., Bryan, R. A., Huang, X., Moadel, T., Schweitzer, A. D., Aisen, P., Nosanchuk, J. D. y Casadevall, A. (2007). Ionizing Radiation Changes the Electronic Properties of Melanin and Enhances the Growth of Melanized Fungi. *PLoS ONE* 2(5): e457. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0000457 — Albert Einstein College of Medicine, Bronx, Nueva York. Hongos: *Cryptococcus neoformans*, *Wangiella dermatitidis*, *Cladosporium sphaerospermum*.
   > "ionizing radiation approximately 500 times higher than background"
   > "Ionizing irradiation changed the electron spin resonance (ESR) signal of melanin"
   > "Melanized fungal cells manifested increased growth relative to non-melanized cells after exposure to ionizing radiation."
   > "produced approximately 2.5 times more CFUs in irradiated melanized cells" (*C. neoformans* H99 melanizado, con nutrientes limitados, 0.05 mGy/h, comparado con células melanizadas sin irradiar)
   > Hipótesis, dicha con cautela: "we cautiously suggest that the ability of melanin to capture electromagnetic radiation … to harness radiation for metabolic energy"
   > "melanized fungal species colonize the walls of the highly radioactive damaged reactor at Chernobyl"
3. Dadachova, E. y Casadevall, A. (2008). Ionizing radiation: how fungi cope, adapt, and exploit with the help of melanin. *Current Opinion in Microbiology* 11(6): 525–531. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2677413/
   > "melanized fungal species colonize the walls of the damaged reactor at Chernobyl"
   > melanin "appears to have significant radioprotective properties"
   > "Melanin pigments are found in all biological kingdoms"
4. Averesch, N. J. H., Shunk, G. K. y Kern, C. (2022). Cultivation of the Dematiaceous Fungus *Cladosporium sphaerospermum* Aboard the International Space Station and Effects of Ionizing Radiation. *Frontiers in Microbiology* 13: 877625. https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2022.877625 (publicado en 2022; el artículo no da porcentaje: reporta 147 vs 151 cuentas por minuto bajo el hongo y bajo el control; por eso la voz dice solo "un poquito menos").
