## Fuentes (cada dato se abrió en el texto completo el 29 oct 2026)

1. Endo, A. (2010). A historical perspective on the discovery of statins. *Proc. Jpn. Acad. Ser. B* 86(5): 484–493. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3108295/
   - Hígado vs. comida: > "In humans, cholesterol produced in the liver exceeds what is absorbed from the diet"
   - 30 pasos y el que marca el ritmo: > "30 enzymatic reactions"; > "The third reaction in the first stages is the committed and rate-limiting step" (la de la HMG-CoA reductasa).
   - La sospecha: > "I speculated that fungi like molds and mushrooms would produce antibiotics that inhibited HMG-CoA reductase."; inspiración: > "Inspired by Fleming's success with molds, we began our project using culture broths of thousands of fungi in April 1971."
   - 3,800 hongos y la citrinina: > "after 3800 strains of fungi, we found that a culture broth of mold showed potent inhibitory activity" … el compuesto era "a known substance—citrinin", abandonado por "its toxicity to the kidneys".
   - El moho del arroz (1972): > "We found a second active culture broth of blue-green mold, Penicillium citrinum Pen-51, which was isolated from a rice sample collected at a grain shop in Kyoto."
   - 1973: > "Finally, in July 1973, we were able to isolate three active metabolites from the culture broth" (el más activo, ML-236B = compactina).
   - Se parece a la pieza de la enzima: > "the structural similarities between compactin and HMG-CoA, the substrate of the HMG-CoA reductase reaction"; > "its mechanism of action, as suggested by its structure, was that of a competitive inhibitor."
   - Monacolina K = lovastatina: > "in February 1979 isolated another statin (named monacolin K) from cultures of Monascus ruber."; > "it was confirmed that monacolin K and mevinolin were the same compound" (mevinolina = lovastatina).
   - Merck y *Aspergillus terreus*: estatina de Merck aislada de "the fungus Aspergillus terreus"; > "lovastatin was given FDA approval to become the first commercial statin in September 1987."
2. Goldstein, J. L. y Brown, M. S. (2015). A Century of Cholesterol and Coronaries: From Plaques to Genes to Statins. *Cell* 161(1): 161–172. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4525717/
   - > "Akira Endo at the Sankyo Company in Tokyo identified the first inhibitor of HMG CoA reductase"; > "Endo's statin, called compactin, was isolated from a penicillium mold."
   - Prima: > "mevinolin, another fungal HMG CoA reductase inhibitor, closely related to compactin."
   - Receptores: > "By blocking cholesterol synthesis, statins lower ER cholesterol, activating SREBP-2" … "the resultant increase in LDL receptors would lower plasma LDL."; > "Most LDL is removed from the circulation by LDL receptors located in the liver."
3. Alberts, A. W. et al. (1980). Mevinolin: a highly potent competitive inhibitor of hydroxymethylglutaryl-coenzyme A reductase and a cholesterol-lowering agent. *PNAS* 77(7): 3957–3961. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC349746/
   - > "Mevinolin, a fungal metabolite, was isolated from cultures of Aspergillus terreus." … "is a potent competitive inhibitor of 3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl-coenzyme A reductase."
4. NCCIH (NIH), "Red Yeast Rice: Usefulness and Safety" (actualizado nov 2022). https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/red-yeast-rice
   - > "Red yeast rice is produced by fermentation of a fungus on rice, usually Monascus purpureus."
   - > "Monacolin K is structurally identical to the medicine lovastatin."
   - "puede traer": la cantidad de monacolina K varía entre productos; con mucha monacolina K tienen "the same potential side effects as statin drugs" y algunos traen citrinina, "toxic and can damage the kidneys". (Por eso el shot no recomienda consumirlo.)
5. Lass-Flörl, C. et al. (2021). *Aspergillus terreus* Species Complex. *Clin. Microbiol. Rev.* 34(4): e00311-20. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8404697/
