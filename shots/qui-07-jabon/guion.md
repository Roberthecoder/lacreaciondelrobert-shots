"Soap, water, and severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2): an ancient handwashing strategy for preventing dissemination of a novel virus." *PeerJ* 9:e12041 (2021). PMID 34616601 · PMC8451441. (Autores de Reckitt, empresa de productos de higiene.)
   > "Soaps are sodium or potassium salts of long-chain (≥8 carbons) fatty acids" · "The formation of soap micelles occurs with hydrophilic (polar head) portions of the molecule extending outward and the hydrophobic (non-polar tail) portions extending inward" · "the ability of the soap micelles to dissolve the non-polar (hydrophobic) portions of the soil load and lipid-enveloped viruses" · "dispersed in the solution by the surfactant, and subsequently are removed with the rinse water" · "The viral inactivation is primarily the consequence of disruption of the lipid envelope by the soap" · "The removal function of soap and water is dependent on lathering and mechanical rubbing" · "the important role played by contaminated hands in dissemination of infectious agents has only been realized since the 19 th century (Semmelweis, 1861)"
2. **Cochrane 2021: 29 ensayos, ≈ 30 % menos episodios de diarrea** — Ejemot-Nwadiaro R.I., Ehiri J.E., Arikpo D., Meremikwu M.M., Critchley J.A. "Hand-washing promotion for preventing diarrhoea." *Cochrane Database Syst Rev* (2021). PMID 33539552 · PMC8094449.

## Notas
- El dibujo es esquemático (la micela real es de unos pocos nanómetros y tiene decenas de moléculas; aquí se dibujan unas cuantas).
- "Algunos virus" = los que tienen envoltura de lípidos (como el SARS-CoV-2); a los virus sin envoltura el jabón los quita con el enjuague, pero no les "desarma" nada: por eso la voz dice "algunos".
