## Fuentes (cada cifra se abrió en la fuente original el 28 oct 2026)

Detalle completo, con la frase textual de cada afirmación, en `verificacion.md`.

1. **965 ha · 112 muestras · 3,810 m · 1,900–8,650 años · fusión = mismo individuo (2003)** — Ferguson, B. A. et al. (2003). *Canadian Journal of Forest Research* 33: 612–623. https://zenodo.org/record/1235905/files/article.pdf
   > "Armillaria ostoyae genet sizes were approximately 20, 95, 195, 260, and 965 ha … The maximum distance between isolates from the 965-ha A. ostoyae genet was approximately 3810 m, and use of three estimates of A. ostoyae spread rate in conifer forests resulted in age estimates for the genet ranging from 1900 to 8650 years."
   > "Sampling of recently dead or live, symptomatic conifers produced 112 isolates of Armillaria"
   > "fusion of opposing mycelia, interpreted as a pairing between the same species and genet"
2. **De raíz en raíz (Ferguson 2003) · cordones negros, brillo, "uno de los más grandes" (2011)** — Baumgartner, K. et al. (2011). *Molecular Plant Pathology* 12(6): 515–534. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6640247/
   > "it colonizes living roots, kills root tissue and then utilizes the dead tissue as its source of nutrition"
   > "this subterranean fungus, which glows in the dark … is one of the largest and oldest organisms on Earth"
2b. **Brillo solo en micelio y rizomorfos** — Ke, H.-M. et al. (2020). *PNAS* 117(49): 31267–31277. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7733832/ ("bioluminescence was only observed in mycelia and rhizomorphs in A. ostoyae")
3. **Cómo se ve la seta** — Matchmaker: Mushrooms of the Pacific Northwest (Servicio Forestal de Canadá), *Armillaria ostoyae*.
4. **Canchas:** cálculo propio, 965 ha ÷ (105 m × 68 m = 0.714 ha) = 1,351.
