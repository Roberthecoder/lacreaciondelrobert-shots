## Fuentes (abiertas el 28 oct 2026; detalle en `verificacion.md`)

1. **Tyrrell, A. R., Reilly, T. y Troup, J. D. (1985).** Circadian variation in stature and the effects of spinal loading. *Spine* 10(2): 161–164. PMID 4002039.
   > "stature was measured with an accuracy of 1 mm in eight young adults. The mean circadian variation was 19.3 mm (1.1% of stature). Fifty-four percent of the diurnal loss in stature occurred in the first hour after rising. Approximately 70% was regained during the first half of the night."
2. **Roberts, N., Hogg, D., Whitehouse, G. H. y Dangerfield, P. (1998).** Quantitative analysis of diurnal variation in volume and water content of lumbar intervertebral discs. *Clinical Anatomy* 11(1): 1–8. Universidad de Liverpool. PMID 9445091.
   > "the mean overnight increase in volume of lumbar discs was 1300 mm3… the change in disc volume is most probably caused by a preferential increase in the water content of the nucleus pulposus."
3. **Paajanen, H. et al. (1994).** Diurnal fluid changes of lumbar discs measured indirectly by magnetic resonance imaging. *J. Orthop. Res.* 12(4): 509–514. PMID 8064481.
   > "The disc signal in T2-weighted magnetic resonance imaging increased as much as 25% after overnight bed rest, presumably due to the enhanced influx of water."
4. **NASA**, "Spinal Ultrasound Investigation" (Estación Espacial Internacional, Expedición 36). https://www.nasa.gov/?p=420143
   > "Medical researchers have observed that crew members grow up to three percent taller during their long duration missions aboard the station and return to their normal height when back on Earth."
5. **Green, D. A. y Scott, J. P. R. (2017).** Spinal health during unloading and reloading associated with spaceflight. *Frontiers in Physiology* 8: 1126. PMC5778142 (revisión).
   > "back pain features frequently in astronaut memoirs, with one stating that it 'comes with the job'"

## Notas
- "Casi dos centímetros" es el **promedio** de 8 jóvenes en 1985 (19.3 mm); varía entre personas (13–21 mm en el estudio de 1994).
- En el gancho, el cambio de estatura del dibujo está exagerado para que se vea.
- "Unos cinco centímetros" es la cuenta 3 % × 1.70 m.
